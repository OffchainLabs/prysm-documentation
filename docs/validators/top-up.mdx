A top-up adds ETH to a validator that already exists. A validator's rewards are proportional to its **effective balance**: a whole number of ETH, capped at the validator's maximum, that follows its actual balance. Top up when:

- **The balance dropped** after downtime penalties. Once the effective balance falls a step, every reward is smaller until you add ETH back. A validator whose effective balance reaches 16 ETH is exited automatically.
- **You want a compounding (`0x02`) validator to earn more.** It can hold up to 2048 ETH, so a top-up grows its rewards without running another validator.

## How much to add

- A **regular (`0x01`)** validator counts at most 32 ETH. The automatic withdrawal sweep sends anything above 32 ETH to its withdrawal address, usually within a few days. Top up only if the balance fell below 32 ETH.
- A **compounding (`0x02`)** validator counts at most 2048 ETH. The automatic withdrawal sweep sends anything above 2048 ETH to its withdrawal address, usually within a few days. Top up only if the balance is below 2048 ETH.

## Add ETH

<When net="mainnet">

To add ETH to an existing validator (at least 1 ETH), open **Validator actions** on the launchpad (<a href={`${mainnet.launchpad}en/validator-actions`}>{`${mainnet.launchpad}en/validator-actions`}</a>), connect your wallet, pick the validator and choose **Add funds**.

</When>
<When net="hoodi">

To add ETH to an existing validator (at least 1 ETH), open the **Top-Up** page of the console ([https://hoodi.console.ethstaker.org/topup](https://hoodi.console.ethstaker.org/topup)), connect your wallet, pick the validator and enter the amount.

</When>
